# Sales and Demographic Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales and Demographic Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The purpose of the project is to analyse business sales performance, customer behaviour, product categories, regional performance and customer segments. The dashboard provides useful insights through interactive visualisations and key performance indicators (KPIs) to support business decision-making.

## Dashboard Preview

The dashboard consists of two interactive pages:

### 1. Sales Performance

This page provides an overview of business sales and profitability.

**Key metrics:**
- Total Sales: 470.53K
- Total Profit: 61.62K
- Profit Margin: 13%
- Total Orders: 2,102

**Visualisations:**
- Sales by product name
- Sales by product category
- Sales by region
- Monthly sales trends

### 2. Demography Performance

This page focuses on customer activity, customer segments and demographic performance.

**Key metrics:**
- Total Customers: 573
- Total Quantity: 7,979
- Unique Orders: 1,038
- Average Sales per Customer: 821.2

**Visualisations:**
- Total quantity by customer
- Sales by customer segment
- Profit by customer name
- Preferred shipping method
- Profit and sales by state

## Key Business Insights

- Furniture recorded the highest sales among the product categories, generating 170.52K.
- The East region recorded the highest regional sales at approximately 156K.
- November recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately 76K.
- Consumer customers contributed the highest sales, generating 266.54K.
- Standard Class was the most preferred shipping method, accounting for 61.27% of orders.
- The leading customer by recorded quantity had 75 units.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query for data transformation and preparation
- DAX for calculated measures and KPIs
- Data visualisation and dashboard design

## Project Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profitability.
- Identify the best-performing products and categories.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Examine monthly sales trends.
- Understand customer segments and purchasing patterns.
- Identify preferred shipping methods.
- Provide actionable business insights.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on maintaining strong performance in the Furniture and Technology categories.
- Investigate opportunities to improve sales in the South region.
- Plan inventory and marketing activities ahead of the year-end sales peak.
- Develop customer retention strategies for the Consumer segment.
- Review delivery preferences to improve customer experience.
